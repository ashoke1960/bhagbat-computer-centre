Role of Computers

Computers help us in many places. They make work easy, fast and accurate.

Main Uses:

  • Schools – teaching and learning
  • Homes – games, study, bills
  • Hospitals – patient records
  • Banks – money transactions
  • Offices – typing and records
  • Railway Stations – ticket booking
  • Shops – billing

Remember:

  • Computer saves time.
  • Computer stores data safely.
  • Computer works quickly.
