Computer - An Electronic Machine

A computer is an electronic machine. It works very fast and helps us in many ways.

Main Points:

  • Computer takes input.
  • Computer processes data.
  • Computer gives output.
  • Computer never gets tired.
  • Computer follows instructions.

Computers and Humans

  • Humans can think.
  • Computer cannot think itself.
  • Computer works faster than humans.
